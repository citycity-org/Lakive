## Flags (vs. June data / July report)

- 🔴 **Toronto +1.6% MoM** (>1% threshold) — $2,537 → $2,577; YoY improved −1.9% → −0.6%. Best-performing major market; listings −6% YoY. 3BR +3.9% YoY ($3,655).
- 🔴 **Vancouver −1.4% MoM** (>1% threshold) — reversed June's gain; YoY −4.5%.
- National YoY narrowed −4.3% → −4.0%; MoM flat at +0.2%. No >5% YoY city moves.
- Calgary YoY improved −5.6% → −4.5%; Montréal YoY worsened −0.8% → −1.6%.
- Ontario apt/condo +0.8% MoM (3rd straight gain from April 46-month low). SK (−0.6%) and MB (−0.8%) slipped MoM — possible easing of interprovincial inflows.
- GTA suburbs (Brampton, Mississauga, Oakville, Oshawa) still −7%+ YoY; Toronto's turnaround has not spread. Markham −11.9%, Côte Saint-Luc −12.9%, Longueuil −10.9%.
- Purpose-built −2.6% YoY ($2,041) most resilient; condo −6.3% ($2,063); houses/townhomes −7.5% ($2,007).

## Key insight

Rents are bottoming: annual declines have narrowed for four straight months and Toronto is nearly flat YoY, but the recovery is concentrated in Toronto's core while Vancouver, Calgary and the GTA suburbs keep falling. Expect the August report to test whether the seasonal peak holds into the fall slowdown.
